# Structured Reasoning

## Objective

Some tasks benefit from breaking the solution into clear intermediate steps before producing the final answer.

---

## Example

### User Prompt

```
A company has a budget of ₹50,000 and wants to purchase laptops costing ₹12,000 each.

How many laptops can be purchased?
```

---

## Structured Solution

1. Identify the available budget.
2. Identify the price per laptop.
3. Divide the budget by the cost of one laptop.
4. Ignore any fractional result because only whole laptops can be purchased.

Final Answer:

```
4 laptops
```

---

## Benefits

- Easier to verify
- Reduces calculation mistakes
- Improves transparency

---

## Best Practice

Use structured intermediate steps for complex reasoning tasks without exposing unnecessary internal reasoning processes.
