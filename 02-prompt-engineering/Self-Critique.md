# Self-Critique Prompting

## Objective

Self-critique prompting asks the AI to identify weaknesses in its own answer and improve it.

---

## Prompt

```
Answer the user's question.

Then identify any possible weaknesses or missing information in your response and provide an improved version.
```

---

## Benefits

- Identifies gaps
- Improves accuracy
- Produces more balanced responses

---

## Evaluation

| Criteria | Result |
|----------|--------|
| Error Detection | High |
| Improvement Potential | High |
| Reliability | Improved |

---

## Best Practice

Use self-critique when accuracy and completeness are more important than producing the fastest possible response.
