# Exploring Multiple Solution Paths

## Objective

Some problems have more than one reasonable approach. Comparing alternatives before selecting a final solution can improve decision quality.

---

## Example

### Task

Design a customer support chatbot.

### Possible Approaches

**Approach A**

Rule-based conversation flow.

Pros

- Simple
- Predictable

Cons

- Limited flexibility

---

**Approach B**

LLM-powered chatbot.

Pros

- Flexible
- Better natural language understanding

Cons

- Higher cost
- Requires evaluation and monitoring

---

## Final Decision

Choose the approach that best satisfies the project's requirements after comparing trade-offs.

---

## Benefits

- Better decision making
- Encourages structured comparison
- Useful for design and planning tasks
